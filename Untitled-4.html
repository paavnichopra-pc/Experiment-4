Healthy Living Tips for Students

Small habits for a healthy life

Food Exercise Sleep Subscribe

Eat Healthy Food

Eat fruits and vegetables every day. Drink enough water and try to eat homemade food.

Do Regular Exercise

Walking or playing for 30 minutes daily helps keep the body active and healthy.

Get Enough Sleep

Students should sleep for 8 to 10 hours every night. Good sleep helps improve memory and concentration.

Quick Tip

Keep your phone away before sleeping.
